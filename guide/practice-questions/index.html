Practice questions with answers

These 24 questions go from finding a molar mass to using it in equations. Try each one on paper first, then open the answer. All answers use the IUPAC abridged atomic weights; with whole-number school values your figure may differ in the last digit, which is fine.

How to use this page

Work in order. Questions 1 to 4 are about counting atoms in a formula. Questions 5 to 11 change between grams, moles and particles. Questions 12 to 17 are on percentage composition and formulas. The rest use molar mass in solutions, gases and equations. If a group goes wrong, the linked guide explains the method.

  1. Find the molar mass of calcium nitrate, Ca(NO3)2.

    Show the answer

    1 Ca, 2 N, 6 O: 40.078 + 2 × 14.007 + 6 × 15.999 = 164.09 g/mol.

  2. Find the molar mass of ammonium phosphate, (NH4)3PO4.

    Show the answer

    3 N, 12 H, 1 P, 4 O: 149.09 g/mol.

  3. Find the molar mass of ethanoic acid, CH3COOH.

    Show the answer

    2 C, 4 H, 2 O: 60.05 g/mol.

  4. Find the molar mass of Mohr's salt, FeSO4·(NH4)2SO4·6H2O.

    Show the answer

    Add the three parts: 151.90 + 132.13 + 6 × 18.015 = 392.12 g/mol.

  5. How many moles are in 36 g of water?

    Show the answer

    36 ÷ 18.02 = 1.998 mol.

  6. How many moles are in 10.6 g of anhydrous sodium carbonate?

    Show the answer

    10.6 ÷ 105.99 = 0.1 mol.

  7. What is the mass of 0.25 mol of glucose?

    Show the answer

    0.25 × 180.16 = 45.04 g.

  8. What is the mass of 3 mol of carbon dioxide?

    Show the answer

    3 × 44.01 = 132 g.

  9. How many molecules are in 8.8 g of carbon dioxide?

    Show the answer

    8.8 ÷ 44.01 = 0.2 mol; × 6.022 × 1023 = 1.204 × 1023 molecules.

  10. How many atoms are in 4 g of helium?

    Show the answer

    4 ÷ 4.003 = 0.9994 mol of atoms; that is 6.018 × 1023 atoms.

  11. Which has more molecules: 10 g of hydrogen or 100 g of oxygen?

    Show the answer

    Hydrogen: 10 ÷ 2.016 = 4.96 mol. Oxygen: 100 ÷ 32.00 = 3.125 mol. The hydrogen has more.

  12. Find the percentage of nitrogen in ammonium nitrate, NH4NO3.

    Show the answer

    2 × 14.007 ÷ 80.04 × 100 = 35.0%.

  13. Find the percentage of oxygen in calcium carbonate.

    Show the answer

    3 × 15.999 ÷ 100.09 × 100 = 48.0%.

  14. Find the percentage of water in Epsom salt, MgSO4·7H2O.

    Show the answer

    7 × 18.015 ÷ 246.47 × 100 = 51.2%.

  15. How much iron is in 1 kg of iron(III) oxide?

    Show the answer

    Iron is 69.94% of Fe2O3, so 1000 g holds 699 g of iron.

  16. A compound has 27.3% carbon and 72.7% oxygen. Find its empirical formula.

    Show the answer

    C: 27.3 ÷ 12.011 = 2.27. O: 72.7 ÷ 15.999 = 4.54. Ratio 1 : 2, so CO2.

  17. A hydrocarbon has the empirical formula CH and a molar mass of about 78 g/mol. Find its molecular formula.

    Show the answer

    CH has a mass of 13.02. 78 ÷ 13.02 = 6, so the formula is C6H6, benzene.

  18. What mass of sodium hydroxide is needed for 500 mL of 0.2 M solution?

    Show the answer

    0.2 × 0.5 × 40.00 = 4 g.

  19. 4.9 g of sulphuric acid is dissolved and made up to 250 mL. Find the molarity.

    Show the answer

    Moles: 4.9 ÷ 98.07 = 0.04996. Molarity: ÷ 0.250 = 0.2 M.

  20. What volume does 8 g of oxygen occupy at 0 °C and 1 atm?

    Show the answer

    8 ÷ 32.00 = 0.25 mol; × 22.4 = 5.6 litres.

  21. A gas has a vapour density of 32. What is its molar mass, and which common gas could it be?

    Show the answer

    Molar mass = 2 × 32 = 64 g/mol. Sulphur dioxide has a molar mass of 64.06.

  22. What mass of carbon dioxide forms when 25 g of calcium carbonate is heated strongly?

    Show the answer

    Moles of CaCO3: 25 ÷ 100.09 = 0.2498. One mole gives one mole of CO2: 0.2498 × 44.01 = 11.0 g.

  23. What mass of water forms when 8 g of methane burns completely? (CH4 + 2O2 → CO2 + 2H2O)

    Show the answer

    Moles of methane: 8 ÷ 16.04 = 0.4987. Water: twice that, 0.9973 mol; × 18.02 = 18.0 g.

  24. 2.86 g of washing soda crystals, Na2CO3·10H2O, are heated until all the water is lost. What mass remains?

    Show the answer

    Moles: 2.86 ÷ 286.14 = 0.009995. Mass of anhydrous salt: × 105.99 = 1.06 g.
