135 compounds met in school, college and the laboratory, each with its molar mass worked out step by step.

The list is arranged by kind of compound: acids, bases, salts, oxides, elements and gases, organic compounds and hydrates. Each entry shows the formula and the molar mass in grams per mole. Search by an everyday name too: "baking soda", "bleach", "fitkari" and "Epsom salt" all work, because each page records the common names of its compound.

Every figure is calculated from the IUPAC abridged atomic weights, never typed by hand, so the list, the pages and the calculator agree to the last digit. School books that use whole-number atomic masses will give answers that differ slightly; each compound page shows both.
