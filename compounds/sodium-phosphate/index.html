Molar mass of sodium phosphate (Na3PO4)

The molar mass of sodium phosphate, Na3PO4, is 163.94 g/mol. One mole of it, which is 6.022 × 1023 formula units, has a mass of 163.94 grams.

163.94 g/mol

3 × 22.990 + 30.974 + 4 × 15.999 = 163.940 · 8 atoms in the formula · also called trisodium phosphate

Open in the calculator

About sodium phosphate

Trisodium phosphate is a strongly alkaline salt used as a cleaning agent and water softener. It is the salt formed when all three acidic hydrogens of phosphoric acid are replaced by sodium.

Other names: trisodium phosphate.

The working

The formula Na3PO4 has 3 sodium atoms, 1 phosphorus atom and 4 oxygen atoms, 8 atoms in all. Multiply each count by the atomic weight of the element and add:

3 × 22.990 + 30.974 + 4 × 15.999 = 163.940 g/mol

With the rounded atomic masses used in many school books (Na = 23, P = 31, O = 16) the answer is 164 g/mol. The difference from 163.94 is too small to matter in most school sums.

Percentage composition

By mass, Na3PO4 is 42.07% sodium, 18.89% phosphorus and 39.04% oxygen. The largest share comes from sodium. In a 100 g sample there are 42.1 g of sodium, 18.9 g of phosphorus and 39.0 g of oxygen.

Worked examples

Grams to moles. How many moles are in 5 g of Na3PO4? Divide the mass by the molar mass: 5 ÷ 163.94 = 0.0305 mol.

Moles to grams. What is the mass of 0.25 mol of Na3PO4? Multiply: 0.25 × 163.94 = 40.98 g.

Counting particles. 5 g contains 0.0305 mol × 6.022 × 1023 = 1.837 × 1022 formula units.

Making a solution. A 0.1 mol/L solution contains 0.1 × 163.94 = 16.39 g of Na3PO4 in each litre. For 250 mL, weigh 4.099 g and make it up to the mark with water.

Questions people ask

What is the molar mass of Na3PO4?

The molar mass of sodium phosphate (Na3PO4) is 163.94 g/mol, or 163.940 g/mol before rounding, using IUPAC abridged atomic weights.

How many moles are in 100 g of Na3PO4?

100 ÷ 163.94 = 0.61 mol.

What is the mass of one formula unit of Na3PO4?

Divide the molar mass by the Avogadro constant: 163.94 ÷ 6.022 × 1023 = 2.722 × 10-22 g.

What is the percentage of sodium in Na3PO4?

Sodium makes up 42.07% of the mass of Na3PO4: 3 × 22.990 ÷ 163.940 × 100.

Which values? This site uses the abridged standard atomic weights published by IUPAC (CIAAW), read on 12 November 2026. Your textbook or question paper may round them differently, for example H = 1 and O = 16, so its answer can differ slightly. In an exam, use the values the paper gives.
