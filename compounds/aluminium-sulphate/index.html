Molar mass of aluminium sulphate (Al2(SO4)3)

The molar mass of aluminium sulphate, Al2(SO4)3, is 342.13 g/mol. One mole of it, which is 6.022 × 1023 formula units, has a mass of 342.13 grams.

342.13 g/mol

2 × 26.982 + 3 × 32.06 + 12 × 15.999 = 342.132 · 17 atoms in the formula · also called aluminum sulfate

Open in the calculator

About aluminium sulphate

Aluminium sulphate is used to clear muddy water and in paper making. Its formula needs care: two aluminium atoms, and a bracket of three sulphate groups giving three sulphur and twelve oxygen atoms.

Other names: aluminum sulfate.

The working

The formula Al2(SO4)3 has 2 aluminium atoms, 3 sulphur atoms and 12 oxygen atoms, 17 atoms in all. Multiply each count by the atomic weight of the element and add:

2 × 26.982 + 3 × 32.06 + 12 × 15.999 = 342.132 g/mol

With the rounded atomic masses used in many school books (Al = 27, S = 32, O = 16) the answer is 342 g/mol. The difference from 342.13 is too small to matter in most school sums.

Percentage composition

By mass, Al2(SO4)3 is 15.77% aluminium, 28.11% sulphur and 56.12% oxygen. The largest share comes from oxygen. In a 100 g sample there are 15.8 g of aluminium, 28.1 g of sulphur and 56.1 g of oxygen.

Worked examples

Grams to moles. How many moles are in 20 g of Al2(SO4)3? Divide the mass by the molar mass: 20 ÷ 342.13 = 0.05846 mol.

Moles to grams. What is the mass of 0.5 mol of Al2(SO4)3? Multiply: 0.5 × 342.13 = 171.1 g.

Counting particles. 20 g contains 0.05846 mol × 6.022 × 1023 = 3.520 × 1022 formula units.

Making a solution. A 0.1 mol/L solution contains 0.1 × 342.13 = 34.21 g of Al2(SO4)3 in each litre. For 250 mL, weigh 8.553 g and make it up to the mark with water.

Questions people ask

What is the molar mass of Al2(SO4)3?

The molar mass of aluminium sulphate (Al2(SO4)3) is 342.13 g/mol, or 342.132 g/mol before rounding, using IUPAC abridged atomic weights.

How many moles are in 100 g of Al2(SO4)3?

100 ÷ 342.13 = 0.2923 mol.

What is the mass of one formula unit of Al2(SO4)3?

Divide the molar mass by the Avogadro constant: 342.13 ÷ 6.022 × 1023 = 5.681 × 10-22 g.

What is the percentage of oxygen in Al2(SO4)3?

Oxygen makes up 56.12% of the mass of Al2(SO4)3: 12 × 15.999 ÷ 342.132 × 100.

Which values? This site uses the abridged standard atomic weights published by IUPAC (CIAAW), read on 12 November 2026. Your textbook or question paper may round them differently, for example H = 1 and O = 16, so its answer can differ slightly. In an exam, use the values the paper gives.
