Molar mass of potassium dichromate (K2Cr2O7)

The molar mass of potassium dichromate, K2Cr2O7, is 294.18 g/mol. One mole of it, which is 6.022 × 1023 formula units, has a mass of 294.18 grams.

294.18 g/mol

2 × 39.098 + 2 × 51.996 + 7 × 15.999 = 294.181 · 11 atoms in the formula · also called potassium dichromate(VI)

Open in the calculator

About potassium dichromate

Potassium dichromate forms bright orange crystals. It is an oxidising agent and a primary standard in redox titrations, turning from orange to green as it reacts. Count carefully: two potassium, two chromium and seven oxygen atoms.

Other names: potassium dichromate(VI).

The working

The formula K2Cr2O7 has 2 potassium atoms, 2 chromium atoms and 7 oxygen atoms, 11 atoms in all. Multiply each count by the atomic weight of the element and add:

With the rounded atomic masses used in many school books (K = 39, Cr = 52, O = 16) the answer is 294 g/mol. The difference from 294.18 is too small to matter in most school sums.

Percentage composition

By mass, K2Cr2O7 is 26.58% potassium, 35.35% chromium and 38.07% oxygen. The largest share comes from oxygen. In a 100 g sample there are 26.6 g of potassium, 35.3 g of chromium and 38.1 g of oxygen.

Worked examples

Grams to moles. How many moles are in 100 g of K2Cr2O7? Divide the mass by the molar mass: 100 ÷ 294.18 = 0.3399 mol.

Moles to grams. What is the mass of 2 mol of K2Cr2O7? Multiply: 2 × 294.18 = 588.4 g.

Counting particles. 100 g contains 0.3399 mol × 6.022 × 1023 = 2.047 × 1023 formula units.

Making a solution. A 0.1 mol/L solution contains 0.1 × 294.18 = 29.42 g of K2Cr2O7 in each litre. For 250 mL, weigh 7.355 g and make it up to the mark with water.

Questions people ask

What is the molar mass of K2Cr2O7?

The molar mass of potassium dichromate (K2Cr2O7) is 294.18 g/mol, or 294.181 g/mol before rounding, using IUPAC abridged atomic weights.

How many moles are in 100 g of K2Cr2O7?

100 ÷ 294.18 = 0.3399 mol.

What is the mass of one formula unit of K2Cr2O7?

Divide the molar mass by the Avogadro constant: 294.18 ÷ 6.022 × 1023 = 4.885 × 10-22 g.

What is the percentage of oxygen in K2Cr2O7?

Oxygen makes up 38.07% of the mass of K2Cr2O7: 7 × 15.999 ÷ 294.181 × 100.

Which values? This site uses the abridged standard atomic weights published by IUPAC (CIAAW), read on 12 November 2026. Your textbook or question paper may round them differently, for example H = 1 and O = 16, so its answer can differ slightly. In an exam, use the values the paper gives.
