Molar mass of magnesium sulphate (MgSO4)

The molar mass of magnesium sulphate, MgSO4, is 120.36 g/mol. One mole of it, which is 6.022 × 1023 formula units, has a mass of 120.36 grams.

120.36 g/mol

24.305 + 32.06 + 4 × 15.999 = 120.361 · 6 atoms in the formula · also called anhydrous magnesium sulfate

Open in the calculator

About magnesium sulphate

Anhydrous magnesium sulphate is a white powder used as a drying agent for organic liquids. With seven molecules of water it is Epsom salt, which has a much higher molar mass.

Other names: anhydrous magnesium sulfate.

The working

The formula MgSO4 has 1 magnesium atom, 1 sulphur atom and 4 oxygen atoms, 6 atoms in all. Multiply each count by the atomic weight of the element and add:

24.305 + 32.06 + 4 × 15.999 = 120.361 g/mol

With the rounded atomic masses used in many school books (Mg = 24, S = 32, O = 16) the answer is 120 g/mol. The difference from 120.36 is too small to matter in most school sums.

Percentage composition

By mass, MgSO4 is 20.19% magnesium, 26.64% sulphur and 53.17% oxygen. The largest share comes from oxygen. In a 100 g sample there are 20.2 g of magnesium, 26.6 g of sulphur and 53.2 g of oxygen.

Worked examples

Grams to moles. How many moles are in 25 g of MgSO4? Divide the mass by the molar mass: 25 ÷ 120.36 = 0.2077 mol.

Moles to grams. What is the mass of 0.1 mol of MgSO4? Multiply: 0.1 × 120.36 = 12.04 g.

Counting particles. 25 g contains 0.2077 mol × 6.022 × 1023 = 1.251 × 1023 formula units.

Making a solution. A 0.1 mol/L solution contains 0.1 × 120.36 = 12.04 g of MgSO4 in each litre. For 250 mL, weigh 3.009 g and make it up to the mark with water.

Questions people ask

What is the molar mass of MgSO4?

The molar mass of magnesium sulphate (MgSO4) is 120.36 g/mol, or 120.361 g/mol before rounding, using IUPAC abridged atomic weights.

How many moles are in 100 g of MgSO4?

100 ÷ 120.36 = 0.8308 mol.

What is the mass of one formula unit of MgSO4?

Divide the molar mass by the Avogadro constant: 120.36 ÷ 6.022 × 1023 = 1.999 × 10-22 g.

What is the percentage of oxygen in MgSO4?

Oxygen makes up 53.17% of the mass of MgSO4: 4 × 15.999 ÷ 120.361 × 100.

Which values? This site uses the abridged standard atomic weights published by IUPAC (CIAAW), read on 12 November 2026. Your textbook or question paper may round them differently, for example H = 1 and O = 16, so its answer can differ slightly. In an exam, use the values the paper gives.
